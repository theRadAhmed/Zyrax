    Before you report

    The gene table or Gene biology panel is empty

    These parts use live public databases (Open Targets, UniProt and STRING). They need an internet connection and can be slow or unavailable for a while. Wait a minute and run the analysis again.

    My lab report filled the wrong value

    The report reader depends on the layout of the report and can miss or misread a value. Always check the filled values before pressing Analyze. Photographs need a sharp, straight, well-lit image.

    Is ZyraX a medical tool?

    No. It cannot diagnose a condition or advise treatment. Please speak to a doctor about any health concern.

    ZyraX is an academic research prototype, not a medical device or diagnostic service. Do not use its outputs for medical decisions.
