About the project

Making biomedical information easier to explore.

Biological measurements and scientific publications can be difficult to interpret in isolation. ZyraX is an academic prototype designed to bring selected information together and help users take the next step in understanding it.

Our goal is to make a research workflow more approachable: review available parameters, examine rule-based disease associations, and discover external research records that may be relevant to a topic.

ZyraX is intended to support learning, exploration and research discussion. Its outputs require scientific interpretation and verification against reliable sources.

01

Enter biomedical parameters

02

Review parameter analysis

The prototype checks supplied values against its configured rules and reports their status.

03

Explore disease associations

Review potential associations generated by predefined rules—not a diagnosis or a probability of disease.

04

Discover research records

Explore records retrieved through the Open Targets research integration and verify the relevance of each result.
